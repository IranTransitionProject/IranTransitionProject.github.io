## Community

engineers, and policy professionals. All repositories are licensed
under MPL 2.0 (code) or CC BY-SA 4.0 (analytical content).

Each repository has its own contribution guidelines:

- [Baseline Contributing Guide](https://github.com/IranTransitionProject/baseline/blob/main/docs/CONTRIBUTING.md)

The project operates under a mission constraint: produce rigorous,
impartial, publicly available analysis. Factional alignment or
institutional capture disqualifies leadership.
